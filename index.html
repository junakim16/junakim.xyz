a little about me

i'm juna. i live in london and work in luxury skincare e-commerce at puig. before that, i spent a year at coty across their luxury fragrance portfolio, and studied business management at king's college london.

i'm passionate about beauty, luxury goods, and the experiences built around them — the rituals, the storytelling, the details that turn a product into something you actually want to live with.

this site is a living document — a place for the things i'm working on, the things i'm into, and whatever else feels worth sharing ✿
